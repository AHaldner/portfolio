## Why waga.gay exists

I got tired of using `example.com` in demos and screenshots, so I finally grabbed a domain I'd wanted
for a long time: **waga.gay**.

## What it looks like

It’s a tiny, silly placeholder site built around the
[`waga` 7tv emote](https://7tv.app/emotes/01JGDM8WNS9YGGVA82KB8HF3NB), with rainbow stripes.

Nothing serious, just a colorful fake URL I can drop into projects when I want something a bit
more “me” than `example.com`.
